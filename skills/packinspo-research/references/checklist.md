# Packaging pre-handoff checklist

- [ ] The direction answers the brief's category, audience and price level, not just a mood.
- [ ] Every borrowed idea cites a Packinspo reference URL; nothing copies a logo, illustration or layout outright.
- [ ] One clear first read on the front panel (brand, product name or variant), not three competing ones.
- [ ] Variant or range system is defined (colour, number, pattern) if more than one SKU is planned.
- [ ] Type choices are licensed or open fonts; lookalikes are labelled as such.
- [ ] Colours are given as targets to proof on the actual substrate and print process.
- [ ] Format and closure are realistic for the product (viscosity, dose, shelf or online).
- [ ] Mandatory label content has space reserved and is flagged for local verification.
- [ ] No health, medical or unverified environmental claims in the copy.
- [ ] Open questions are listed rather than guessed.
